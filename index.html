CARPET CARE

Carpet Care Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

Handcrafted rugs are made to last generations. Here is how to preserve their luster, along with answers to our most common client inquiries.

Artisan Care & Longevity Guide

Gentle Weekly Vacuuming

Vacuum once a week on suction-only mode. Keep rotating beater bars away from delicate fringe edges and silk piles.

Immediate Spill Emergency Protocol

Never scrub! Blot instantly using a clean, undyed white cotton towel. Use lukewarm water with mild natural wool shampoo if required.

180° Semi-Annual Rotation

Rotate your rug 180 degrees every 6 months to ensure foot traffic and ambient daylight age the fibers into an even, beautiful patina.

Professional Organic Luster Wash

Have your carpet professionally washed every 3 to 5 years by traditional rug washing specialists to refresh natural lanolin oils.
