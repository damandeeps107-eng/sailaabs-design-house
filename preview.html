EXPERT GUIDANCE

How to Choose the Right Rug for Your Home

Choosing the perfect rug can transform your room. Here are 4 key factors to consider before ordering.

01

Measurement & Proportions

Ensure all front legs of your sofa sit on the rug. For dining rooms, choose a rug that extends 24 inches beyond the table on all sides.

02

Price & Investment Value

Hand-knotted rugs are heirloom investments that last 50+ years and increase in character. Hand-tufted rugs offer plush everyday luxury.

03

Footfall & Room Traffic

High-traffic living rooms and hallways benefit from dense New Zealand wool flatweaves. Low-traffic bedrooms suit plush silk shaggy rugs.

04

Fibre Strength & Texture

Pure wool offers natural stain resilience and springiness. Bamboo silk delivers a lustrous sheen. Jute adds rustic organic warmth.

CARE & FAQ

Carpet Care & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom ordering, loom production, knot density, and maintenance.

What is the lead time for a custom hand-knotted or hand-tufted rug?

Hand-tufted rugs typically take 2–3 weeks from order approval. Fine hand-knotted silk & wool carpets require 4–6 weeks depending on size and knot density (KPSI) because every knot is woven by hand on Bhadohi looms.

Can I request a custom size, color match, or material swatch?

Yes. We specialize in custom made-to-order manufacturing. We can weave any size (up to 20ft wide), match yarn swatches to your interior paint or fabric samples, and provide physical strike-offs before loom production.

How do I clean and maintain a handmade wool or silk rug?

Vacuum regularly using a suction-only vacuum head (avoid rotary beater bars on silk). For liquid spills, blot immediately with a clean dry cloth — do not rub. Professional organic hand-washing is recommended every 3–4 years.

Do you ship carpets across India and internationally?

Yes, we provide insured door-to-door transit across all major Indian cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Jaipur, Kolkata) and international courier dispatch for global orders.

Why buy directly from Sailaab’s Design House in Bhadohi?

Bhadohi is India’s carpet weaving capital. Buying directly from our workshop looms eliminates 30–50% retail showroom markups, ensuring authentic artisan quality, transparent pricing, and direct communication.
