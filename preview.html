RUG BUYING GUIDE

How to Choose the Right Rug for Your Home

Essential considerations to keep in mind when investing in a fine handmade carpet.

01

Measurement & Placement

Measure your seating area carefully. In living rooms, aim to place at least the front two legs of all sofas on the carpet for a cohesive aesthetic.

02

Foot Traffic & Room Function

Select plush wool flatweaves or hand-tufted piles for high-traffic corridors and living areas, while reserving delicate Kashmiri silk rugs for bedrooms.

03

Material Resilience

Pure New Zealand wool provides natural stain resistance and elasticity, while silk adds unparalleled sheen and high knot density detail.

04

Knotted vs Tufted Weave

Hand-knotted rugs are generation-lasting heirloom investments, whereas hand-tufted rugs offer rich plushness and quicker lead times.
