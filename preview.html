OUR STORY

Rooted in Bhadohi.
Established 1994

Sailaab’s Design House works from the weaving traditions of Bhadohi — one of India’s principal carpet-producing regions. We make rugs for homes, hospitality projects and interior commissions.

Bhadohi, India

Woven by skilled artisans in Bhadohi, India.

Made by Hand, in Bhadohi

Each piece is woven on traditional vertical looms by artisans who have worked in this craft for years. The process is slow and intentional — knot by knot, row by row.

We work directly with weaving teams in Bhadohi and offer made-to-order rugs without the margins of retail distribution.

CARE & FAQ

Carpet Care & Common Questions

A well-maintained rug lasts for many years. Below are care guidelines and answers to the questions we hear most often.

Natural Fiber Longevity

Wool and silk fibres develop character over time with proper care.

Flip the carpet over. On an authentic hand-knotted carpet, the exact design and colors are mirrored clearly on the reverse side with individual knot loops visible. There is zero synthetic latex backing, glue, or mesh applied.

Gentle Weekly Vacuuming

Suction-only mode. Keep revolving beater bars away from delicate fringe edges.

Immediate Spill Care

Blot instantly with a dry white cotton towel. Never scrub vigorously.

Rotate Every Six Months

Turn 180° semi-annually to balance traffic pathways and sunlight exposure.

Professional Wash Every 3–5 Yrs

Traditional organic washing restores natural lanolin and pile vibrancy.
