THE STORY BEHIND SAILAAB'S DESIGN HOUSE

Three Decades of Weaving Heritage

Founded in 1994 in Bhadohi — Asia’s premier carpet weaving center — Sailaab’s Design House represents over 30 years of authentic craftsmanship. Our master weavers hand-knot and hand-tuft every rug using 100% pure New Zealand wool, Kashmir silk, and eco-friendly organic fibers.

With our corporate showroom in Shivpur, Varanasi, we provide interior designers, architects, and homeowners direct access to bespoke, loom-priced handmade carpets without middleman markups.
